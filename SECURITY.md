# Security policy

**Please don't report security problems in public issues.**

Email **security@joinoasa.org** with:

- What you found and where
- Steps to reproduce it
- What you think the impact is
- How to reach you, if you want a reply

You can also use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the affected repository, where it's turned on.

In scope: joinoasa.org and OASA's GitHub repositories, published designs and firmware. Third-party platforms are out of scope; report those to the platform.

Ground rules: don't access other people's data, don't degrade the service, and give us time to fix the issue before you publish it. Good-faith research is welcome. Full policy: https://joinoasa.org/security/
