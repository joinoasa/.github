# Code of conduct

The full code of conduct is at **https://joinoasa.org/docs/code-of-conduct/**. It's a plain-language draft pending attorney review.

In short:

1. Respect everyone. No harassment or discrimination.
2. Safety first: people, property, airspace and waterways.
3. Follow the law and OASA security rules.
4. Protect youth members ([youth rules](https://joinoasa.org/docs/membership/#youth-members-15-to-17)).
5. Keep member and contract information confidential.

**Raise a concern:** tell your project or chapter lead, or email hello@joinoasa.org with "Conduct" in the subject.
